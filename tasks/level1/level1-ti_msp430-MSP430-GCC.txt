[Blink_1Hz_LED]
Blink the onboard LED2 at 1 Hz.
Use pin p1.1 for the LED2.

[Blink_4Hz_LED]
Blink the onboard LED1 at 4 Hz.
Use pin p1.0 for the LED1.

[Botton_to_LED]
Read the state of a button (switch s2), and turn on LED1 when the button switch s2 is pressed. 
Use GPIO p5.5 for the button and GPIO p1.0 for the LED1.

[Blink_0.5Hz_LED]
Blink the onboard LED1 at 0.5 Hz.
Use GPIO p1.0 for the LED1.
